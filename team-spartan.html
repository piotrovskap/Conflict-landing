Before we get started

Scope, access, delivery, and what happens after the assessment.

01What do I get from a security assessment?

An agreed scope, findings with supporting evidence, a prioritized remediation plan, and a quote for the implementation you want us to take on. We confirm the systems, deliverables, timeline, and price before work starts.

02Will you fix what you find?

Yes. We can implement the changes you approve and re-test the affected paths under a separate remediation scope. If you need independent assurance, we arrange independent assessment rather than certify our own work.

03Do you need access to production?

We agree the access needed for your scope first. We start with read-only access where practical and agree approvals, a change window, and rollback plans before production changes. Keep credentials and sensitive incident details out of the public form and site chat.

04How quickly can you help?

Scheduled assessments start on an agreed date. The $5K triage offer has a six-business-hour response window, Monday–Friday. Other incident packages and retainers have their own coverage and response terms. Contact us to confirm off-hours availability.

05Can you review an AI agent or LLM application?

Yes. We review permissions, tool integrations, retrieval, data exposure, and runtime controls. Specialist adversarial assessment is scoped separately; specialist lead availability is typically two to four weeks. We explain the methods, staffing, and limits before you commit.

06What if the fix needs application code changes?

CONFLICT, the technology studio behind Team Spartan, handles application security and product engineering. We scope that work explicitly and coordinate the handoff so infrastructure and code changes fit together.

07Do you take over our systems?

We work alongside your team. Your organization keeps control of its systems, repositories, and accounts. The handover includes the agreed evidence, configuration changes, and operating guidance.

08How does pricing work?

Assessments and remediation are scoped before they are priced. Ongoing support has published retainers and hour packs. Monthly retainer hours do not roll over. We confirm coverage, onboarding, and any response SLA in your engagement terms.
